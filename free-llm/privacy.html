Free LLM privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Content and permissions

The app processes your messages, attached photos and image prompts on your device. It requests camera access when you choose to take a photo. Your chats and images are stored locally. You can delete a conversation in chat history.

Copies you export or share are managed by the destination you choose. Device backups are governed by your Apple settings. You can change microphone, camera and notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Advertising

The free app uses Google AdMob to request non-personalized ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can process device identifiers, coarse location inferred from your IP address, ad and app interactions, crash logs and performance data for ad delivery, measurement and diagnostics. The app does not submit your audio, photos, prompts or chat contents to Google.

Where required, the app obtains consent before requesting ads and provides ad privacy choices. Google handles its data under its privacy policy, which describes its safeguards, retention and deletion options. Third parties handling app data must provide protections consistent with this policy and applicable platform requirements.

Purchases

Apple processes Lifetime access payments. The app checks and restores your purchase with StoreKit. Your payment card details stay with Apple.

Privacy questions and deletion requests

Use the in-app deletion controls for your local content. For other privacy questions, consent requests or help with data deletion, contact kirstenali355@gmail.com.

Report an ad

If an ad seems inappropriate or unsuitable for this app’s age rating, use the report option in the ad menu when available or send an ad report to support.
